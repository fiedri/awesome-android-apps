# Alternatives to Google apps

> Google apps are closed source, track you and feed their advertising business.
> Here is a list of open, privacy-friendly alternatives that are just as good —
> or better. Every alternative links to its source repository so you can build
> it yourself or download it from its store page.

| Google app | Alternative(s) |
|------------|----------------|
| Play Store | [F-Droid](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroidclient), [Aurora Store](https://gitlab.com/AuroraOSS/AuroraStore), [Aurora Droid](https://gitlab.com/AuroraOSS/auroradroid) |
| YouTube | [NewPipe](https://github.com/TeamNewPipe/NewPipe), [LibreTube](https://github.com/libre-tube/LibreTube) |
| Gmail | [K-9 Mail (Thunderbird)](https://github.com/thunderbird/thunderbird-android) |
| Files | [Material Files](https://github.com/zhanghai/MaterialFiles) |
